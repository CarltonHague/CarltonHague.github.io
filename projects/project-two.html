About the project

This project was a short game development challenge where we built a Flappy Bird-inspired game in C++ using SFML. The goal was to improve our C++ skills and to be produce a working 2d game demo in a short time frame.

What I worked on

  • I worked on the player movement and score progression systems.
  • I worked on the general code structure and implemented an obejct oriented approach to our code base through the use of standard class and object design.
  • One challenge that I had to overcome was ensuring the player movement felt smooth, fair and responsive. for example the rotation of the sprite had to correctly match the Y velocity and making sure the player snaped back up when the player pressed space to increase height. I also placing caps on the players max rotation to ensure they could not double back on themselves or spin out of control. Creating a gravity like downwards force on the player meant implementing a max terminal velocity to the player as well as a constant gravity coefficent that acts on the player pulling them downwards. I also implemented player animations that play for a few frames after the player presses space giving a flapping animation to the bird.
