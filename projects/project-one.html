About the project

The game was a first-year group project, made in Unity to fit a brief for a mascot platformer with two levels, enemies and collectables. It was showcased at the university’s annual games show.

What I worked on

  • I was responsible for implementing most of the player systems, including movement, jumping, dashing and grappling. I also implemented the animations for the player and enemies, and created a lot of the gameplay feel.
  • One of the main challenges on this project was implementing the grappling and dashing mechanics. The designers wanted the characters to be able to air dash through gaps and grapple to specific points, and tuning that movement made a big difference to the game’s flow.
  • I learned a lot from this project, as it was my first time using Unity and working in a team alongside artists and designers. I took on the role of Scrum master for the group, leading our weekly meetings and keeping the project on schedule.
