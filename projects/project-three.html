About the project

This was a solo Unity project built over 3 weeks. I wanted to recreate the feel of a classic Space Invaders experience with responsive movement, wave-based enemy pressure, and a simple but engaging arcade loop.

What I worked on

  • I built the main shooting, movement, and enemy wave systems to recreate the feel of a classic space invaders game.
  • I implemented the enemy behaviour, difficulty progression, and score systems to keep each run engaging and fair.
  • I focused on polishing the gameplay feel so the project was smooth, fast, and fun to play within the 3-week development window.
