Colour Changing Space Invaders

A classic space invaders style shooter with a twist: the player can switch colours to damage matching enemies and avoid taking damage from enemies of the same colour.

Role
Gameplay programmer
Tools
Unity, C#
Team
Solo
Timeframe
4 weeks

About the project

This was a solo Unity project built over 4 weeks. I wanted to create a classic space invaders gameplay loop with a new mechanic that added decision-making and depth. The player can change colour with right click, and enemies are only vulnerable when they are the opposite colour to the player. Enemies of the same colour can’t damage the player, which encourages timing, positioning, and careful use of colour switching.

What I worked on

  • I built the main shooting, movement, and enemy wave systems to recreate the feel of a classic space invaders game.
  • I implemented the colour mechanic, including colour switching, enemy damage rules, and the player’s defensive interaction with matching enemies.
  • I also focused on polishing the game feel by balancing the enemy patterns, projectile behaviour, and difficulty progression over the full 4-week development period.
