Colour Changing Space Invaders

A classic space invaders-inspired shooter with a colour-switching mechanic, where the player can only damage enemies of the opposite colour and must survive a mothership-style threat.

Role
Gameplay programmer
Tools
Unity, C#
Team
Solo
Timeframe
4 weeks

About the project

This was a solo Unity project built over 4 weeks. It takes the classic space invaders formula and adds a simple but impactful twist: the player can change colour by right-clicking, and only enemies of the opposite colour can be damaged. Matching colours are harmless to the player, so the game becomes a constant balancing act between offence, defence, and positioning.

To add extra pressure, I also introduced a mothership-style enemy with unique attack patterns that restrict the player’s movement and force them to react quickly under pressure.

What I worked on

  • I built the core shooting, movement, enemy wave, and score systems to recreate the feel of a classic space invaders game.
  • I implemented the colour-switch mechanic, including enemy damage rules, collision logic, and the player’s defensive interactions with matching enemies.
  • I designed and implemented a mothership-style enemy with special attack patterns that disrupt movement and force the player to adapt their strategy.
  • I focused on balancing the gameplay loop so each encounter felt readable, fair, and skill-based while still being fast and satisfying to play.
